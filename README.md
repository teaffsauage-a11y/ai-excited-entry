One image that gets an AI judge excited: a tiny astronaut in a white spacesuit surfing on a giant glowing golden koi fish as it leaps out of sparkling water over a neon city skyline at dawn. The scale is absurd, the momentum is real, and the whole scene reads at a glance.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, defaulting to `/output/entry.png`.
